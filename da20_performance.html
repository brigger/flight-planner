RPM to set is from the aircraft checklist (the real data, ISA) — the checklist and POH Table 3 use different RPM references, so the speed & fuel are taken from POH Table 3 at the chosen %bhp, while the RPM shown is the checklist value you actually dial in. Fuel converted from US GPH to litres/hour (× 3.785). Temperature columns: −20 °C / standard / +10 °C. Verify against the POH before flight.

Descent (Route Planner): modelled at 500 ft/min at 2000 RPM. 2000 RPM is below Table 3's tabulated range, so descent TAS & fuel flow are extrapolated from Table 3 (not verbatim).
